# Articles

### Get started

- [Introduction to
  mctq](https://docs.ropensci.org/mctq/articles/mctq.md):

  This article takes a quick tour of all standard Munich ChronoType
  Questionnaire (MCTQ) main functions from the `mctq` package.

### Notes

- [Dealing with missing
  sections](https://docs.ropensci.org/mctq/articles/missing-sections.md):

  This article shows a possible workaround to deal with missing sections
  when working with a standard or micro Munich ChronoType Questionnaire
  (MCTQ).

- [The social jetlag
  computation](https://docs.ropensci.org/mctq/articles/sjl-computation.md):

  This article provides notes on different approaches to computing
  social jetlag for the Munich ChronoType Questionnaire (MCTQ). It also
  explains how the ‘method’ argument in the ‘sjl_rel()’, ‘sjl()’,
  ‘sjl_sc_rel()’, and ‘sjl_sc()’ functions works.
